Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains how Keen Resources Ltd (trading as Keen Resources and Keen Clean, "we", "us") collects and uses your personal information when you visit our website, ask for a quote, contact us or use our services. We are based in London, United Kingdom.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Keen Resources Ltd is the data controller. You can contact us about this policy or your data at info@keenresources.co.uk or on +44 7940 713239.

2. What information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address and property address or postcode.
  • Enquiry and job details: the service you ask about (for example cleaning, man and van, building work), property type and size, preferred dates, access notes, photos or videos you send us, and the quotes, bookings and invoices we create for you.
  • Communications: emails, WhatsApp and chat messages, text messages, and notes or transcripts of phone calls with us.
  • Payment records: invoice amounts, payment dates and references. We do not store your card details.
  • Advert and website data: if you reply to one of our Facebook or Instagram adverts, the details you enter in the form. When you use our website we may collect technical data such as your IP address, device, browser and the pages you visit, through cookies and similar tools.

4. Automated tools and AI

To answer enquiries quickly we use automated tools, including an automated phone assistant, a website chat assistant and AI tools that help us draft replies. Calls handled by the phone assistant may be transcribed. A member of our team reviews messages and quotes before they are sent to you, and we do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects using automated means alone. Ask for a person at any time and we will take over.

5. Who we share your information with

We only share what is needed, with providers that process data on our behalf, such as:

  • Email, calendar and document services (Google Workspace);
  • Customer relationship, messaging and job-management tools (for example HubSpot, Chatwoot and Jobber);
  • Telephony, text-message, voice and automation services (for example Twilio, Vapi, ElevenLabs and n8n);
  • AI service providers used to draft replies and answer routine questions;
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) for lead forms from our adverts, and WhatsApp for messages you send us;
  • Google Analytics and similar tools for website statistics;
  • Our accountants, payment and banking providers, insurers, and subcontractors or partners who carry out part of a job for us (we give them only the details they need to do it).

We may also share information where the law requires it, or to protect our rights. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Transfers outside the UK

Some of our providers are based outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens we rely on UK-approved safeguards such as adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or equivalent protections.

7. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and quotes that do not become a job: up to 24 months.
  • Jobs, invoices and payment records: 6 years after the end of the tax year, for accounting and tax rules.
  • Call transcripts, chat logs and photos you send: up to 24 months, or longer if needed for a complaint or claim.
  • Marketing contacts: until you ask us to stop.

8. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies so it works, and analytics and customer-relationship cookies (for example Google Analytics and HubSpot) to understand how visitors use it and to follow up enquiries. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access your information, correct it, ask us to delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, move it to another provider, and withdraw consent at any time. To use any of these rights, email info@keenresources.co.uk. We will reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113, although we would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

10. Security

We use access controls, passwords and encrypted connections to protect your information, and we limit who can see it. No system is completely secure, so please do not send us sensitive information such as full card numbers by email or chat.

11. Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the date at the top.
